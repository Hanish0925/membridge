The CLI

membridge 
Usage: membridge [OPTIONS] COMMAND [ARGS]...

Migrate agent memory between stores, and measure what survived.

Commands
  schema   Check, or create, the CMM schema in a CockroachDB target.
  bundles  List the migration bundles held in a target.
  migrate  Mem0 -> CMM -> CockroachDB, then read it back and score it.
  search   Semantic recall straight against the store, with no model in the way.
  ask      Ask the agent, and see which memories it used to answer.

$ membridge ask "what are our favorite restaurants and food preferences?" --user-id john_001
  recall('favorite restaurants food preferences dietary likes dislikes cuisine') -> 5 memories
    [0.506] Our favourite restaurant for a night out is Casa Lupita on Bell Street.
    [0.451] I cannot eat shrimp, crab, lobster or anything cooked in the same fryer.
    ...

[OK] Your favorite restaurant for a night out is Casa Lupita on Bell Street. For food
preferences, you avoid shellfish and dislike coriander, while Maria eats a vegetarian diet.

Five commands, one claim: what you migrate is what you can measure.
